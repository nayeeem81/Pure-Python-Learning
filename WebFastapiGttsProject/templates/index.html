Text-to-speech generation

Per-word audio editing

After speech is generated, users can adjust each word individually using: Tone (Pitch Shift) controls Amplitude (Volume dB) controls

Word control grid: The current sentence is broken into individual words, each with its own pitch and volume sliders.

Some words have non-default settings, such as: “welcome” with a pitch shift of 0.2 and volume of 13 dB “to” with a pitch shift of 0.5 and volume of 12 dB Example sentence: The workspace is loaded with a sample greeting beginning, “Hello, welcome to the Center of Command (GTTS, DSP)...” and explains that users can type sentences, convert them to audio, and then adjust tone and volume word-by-word.
